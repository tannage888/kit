description: Get a reconnection script for a dormant contact
---

Get a reconnection brief and compose an opener for a contact you've lost touch with.

Usage: `/kit-reconnect <name>`

$ARGUMENTS

Call `kit-reconnect-context` with contact_name: "$ARGUMENTS" and present the results.

Then compose a warm, brief reconnection message that:
1. Acknowledges the gap naturally (not apologetically)
2. References something specific from your history or their interests
3. Keeps it low-pressure — opens the door without demanding a response
4. Is appropriate for their preferred channel

Present the draft and offer to adjust the tone or length.
