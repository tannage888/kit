description: Pre-flight brief before a conversation — background, interests, open follow-ups
---

Get a conversation prep card for a contact before reaching out.

Usage: `/kit-prep <name>`

$ARGUMENTS

Call `kit-prep-card` with contact_name: "$ARGUMENTS" and present the results.

After showing the prep card, offer to:
- Draft a message with `/kit-draft <name>` if they want to reach out
- Log an interaction afterward with `/kit-update <name>`
